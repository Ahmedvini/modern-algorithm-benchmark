## Known measurement limitations and confounders

Nanosecond units do not imply nanosecond accuracy; timer overhead is not subtracted. No direct per-query maxima, isolated structure memory, end-to-end index costs, confidence intervals or significance tests. No comparison or memory figures are fabricated from timing data.
